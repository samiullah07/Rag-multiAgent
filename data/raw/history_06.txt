# history - Document 6

This document covers various aspects of history.

- World War II ended in 1945 with the unconditional surrender of Germany in May and Japan in September.

- The Berlin Wall fell in 1989 on November 9th, marking a pivotal moment in ending Cold War divisions.

- Christopher Columbus reached the Americas in 1492 while sailing under the Spanish crown.

- The Declaration of Independence was signed in 1776 on July 4th in Philadelphia.

- The French Revolution began in 1789 with the storming of the Bastille on July 14th.

- Napoleon Bonaparte was defeated at Waterloo in 1815 by the allied forces led by Wellington.

- World War I started in 1914 after the assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand in Sarajevo.

- The Treaty of Versailles was signed in 1919 officially ending World War I.

- The Magna Carta was signed in 1215 at Runnymede, limiting English royal authority.

- The Industrial Revolution began in Britain around 1760 with the advent of mechanized manufacturing.
