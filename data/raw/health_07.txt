# health - Document 7

This document covers various aspects of health.

- The average human heart beats about 100,000 times per day throughout a lifetime.

- A typical adult has a blood volume of approximately 5 liters circulating through the body.

- The human brain uses about 20% of the body's total energy consumption at rest.

- Vitamin C deficiency leads to scurvy, a disease that plagued sailors until the 18th century.

- The first vaccine was developed by Edward Jenner for smallpox in 1796 using cowpox.

- Antibiotics revolutionized medicine after the discovery of penicillin in 1928.

- The Human Genome Project was completed in 2003 after 13 years and  billion investment.

- COVID-19 was first identified in Wuhan, China in December 2019 as a novel coronavirus.

- The average life expectancy in Japan is 84.6 years, among the highest globally.

- Regular exercise can reduce the risk of heart disease by up to 35% according to studies.

This document provides key information about health suitable for educational and reference purposes.
