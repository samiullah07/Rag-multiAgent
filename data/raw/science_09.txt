# science - Document 9

This document covers various aspects of science.

- The speed of light in vacuum is 299,792,458 meters per second. This is a fundamental physical constant.

- The Earth orbits the Sun at an average distance of 149.6 million kilometers. This distance is defined as one astronomical unit.

- Water boils at 100 degrees Celsius at sea level under standard atmospheric pressure.

- The human body contains approximately 37.2 trillion cells, according to recent cellular biology estimates.

- DNA was first discovered by Friedrich Miescher in 1869 while studying white blood cells.

- The periodic table was created by Dmitri Mendeleev in 1869, organizing elements by atomic weight and properties.

- Albert Einstein published the theory of relativity in 1905 in his annus mirabilis papers.

- The first antibiotic penicillin was discovered by Alexander Fleming in 1928 at St. Mary's Hospital.

- The structure of DNA was determined by Watson and Crick in 1953 using X-ray crystallography data.

- The first human heart transplant was performed by Christiaan Barnard in 1967 in Cape Town, South Africa.

This document provides key information about science suitable for educational and reference purposes.
