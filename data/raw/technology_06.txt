# technology - Document 6

This document covers various aspects of technology.

- The first iPhone was released by Apple in 2007 on January 9th at Macworld conference.

- The World Wide Web was invented by Tim Berners-Lee in 1989 while at CERN.

- The first email was sent by Ray Tomlinson in 1971 to himself as a test message.

- Google was founded by Larry Page and Sergey Brin in 1998 in a garage in Menlo Park.

- Facebook was launched by Mark Zuckerberg in 2004 originally for Harvard students.

- The first microprocessor, the Intel 4004, was introduced in 1971 revolutionizing computing.

- The first computer mouse was invented by Douglas Engelbart in 1964 for human-computer interaction.

- The first commercial microprocessor was the Intel 4004 released in 1971 with 2,300 transistors.

- The first domain name symbolics.com was registered in 1985 starting the commercial internet.

- The first SMS message was sent in 1992 saying 'Merry Christmas' on the Vodafone network.
