# geography - Document 1

This document covers various aspects of geography.

- Mount Everest is the highest mountain on Earth at 8,848.86 meters above sea level.

- The Pacific Ocean covers approximately 165.25 million square kilometers making it the largest ocean.

- The Nile River is the longest river in the world, stretching about 6,650 kilometers.

- The Amazon Rainforest produces about 20% of the world's oxygen through photosynthesis.

- The Sahara Desert covers about 9.2 million square kilometers across North Africa.

- The Great Wall of China is approximately 21,196 kilometers long winding across northern China.

- The Statue of Liberty was a gift from France to the United States in 1886.

- Australia is both a country and a continent, covering 7.69 million square kilometers.

- The Mariana Trench is the deepest part of the world's oceans, reaching 11,034 meters deep.

- The Great Barrier Reef is the largest coral reef system, stretching over 2,300 kilometers.
